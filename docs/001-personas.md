## Personas

### Creator

**Role:** Person who creates and publishes cards

**Goals:**
- Create a shareable, structured representation of something (self, project, collectible)
- Maintain ownership and version history
- Control how the card is used (DRM/terms)

**Context:**
- Mobile-first, web-native
- May be technical or non-technical
- Wants portability - not locked into a platform

**Pain Points:**
- Data scattered across platforms with no single source of truth
- No ownership of own profile data on social platforms
- Sharing project specs via unstructured docs (email, PDFs)

**Success Criteria:**
- Card is easily created, updated, and shared via URL
- Clear provenance and version history

---

### Viewer

**Role:** Person who views/consumes cards

**Goals:**
- Quickly understand the card's content
- Trust the source (verify ownership/authenticity)
- Take action (contact, quote, trade)

**Context:**
- Receives card via link or embed
- Mobile or desktop
- May want to create a card in response

**Pain Points:**
- Receiving outdated or inconsistent information
- No way to verify authenticity
- Friction to respond or engage

**Success Criteria:**
- Card renders clearly on any device
- Can verify ownership/version
- Easy path to action

---

### Claimant

**Role:** Person claiming ownership of a card created by someone else

**Goals:**
- Take authoritative ownership of a card about themselves
- Merge/reconcile with existing cards they own

**Context:**
- Someone else created a card about them (e.g., a fan-made profile)
- Wants to establish themselves as the authority

**Pain Points:**
- Multiple cards exist describing the same entity
- No clear provenance or ownership transfer mechanism

**Success Criteria:**
- Clear claim/verification flow
- Ability to merge or supersede existing cards
